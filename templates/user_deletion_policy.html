Account Deletion Policy

Last updated: September 2026  |  Powered by NexisCodeLabs

📋 Overview

Mithran ERP is a school management platform used by educational institutions to manage students, staff, academics, fees, and parent communication. This policy explains how users can request deletion of their account and what happens to their data when they do.

Account deletion requests are processed within 30 days. Some data may be retained for legal, financial, or academic compliance as described below.

👤 Who Can Request Account Deletion

  • Students / Parents — may request deletion of their student portal account.
  • Staff members — may request deletion of their staff login account.
  • School administrators — may delete accounts of students and staff within their institution.

Account deletion by an individual user does not delete institutional records maintained by the school. The school (data controller) manages records under its own data retention obligations.

🗑️ How to Request Account Deletion

You can request account deletion in any of the following ways:

  • Via the Mobile App — Open the Mithran ERP app → go to Profile → Settings → Delete My Account and confirm your request.

  • Via Email — Send a deletion request from your registered email address to madhan.dorai@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and include your registered username or mobile number.

  • Via Your School Administrator — Contact your school's admin office and ask them to deactivate or delete your account from the ERP portal.

🔒 What Data Is Deleted vs. Retained

Upon account deletion, the following actions are taken:

Data Type Action Reason
Login credentials (username & password) Deleted Access is revoked immediately
Device FCM / push notification tokens Deleted Notifications are stopped
App session data & preferences Deleted No longer needed after deletion
Academic records (marks, attendance) Retained Required by educational regulations & school records
Fee payment history & receipts Retained Financial audit and legal compliance
Payroll / salary records (staff) Retained Tax and statutory compliance
Personal profile details (name, contact) Retained Maintained by the institution as part of school records
Retained data is held by the school (as the data controller) and is not accessible via the app after account deletion.

⏱️ Deletion Timeline

  • Login access is disabled immediately upon confirmation of the deletion request.
  • Push notification tokens and session data are removed within 24 hours.
  • Full account deletion is completed within 30 days of the request.
  • Backup copies may persist for up to 90 days before being purged from backup systems.

🔄 Account Reactivation

If you change your mind, you may request reactivation within 30 days of submitting the deletion request, before the deletion is processed. After the 30-day window, reactivation is not possible and a new account would need to be created by your institution.

Contact your school administrator or email us at madhan.dorai@gmail.com to cancel a pending deletion request.
